Why Use PLC Intake Tracker?

🔒 Secure & Private

All data is stored 100% locally on your machine. Features a 4-digit PIN login to protect sensitive client information.

📋 Audit Trails

Track every action. View a full timeline of status changes, call outcomes, and completed tasks for compliance.

📊 Analytics Dashboard

Monitor your daily performance with real-time stats on Active Clients, Scheduled Tasks, Overdue items, and Completed tasks.

🔄 Intake Pipeline

Track clients through their complete lifecycle: New Referral, Screening, Scheduled, Completed, Missed, and Closed.

🔊 Custom Alarms

Never miss a callback. Set your own custom sound file to play 10 minutes before a task is due.

🖨️ Print & Export

Generate printable daily call sheets for your desk or export your task data directly to CSV for Excel reporting.

📞 Quick Contact

Direct "Dial" and "Email" buttons that open your default calling and mailing apps instantly.

🎨 Modern UI

Built with a sleek, dark-mode Windows 11 aesthetic that is easy on the eyes during long shifts.
